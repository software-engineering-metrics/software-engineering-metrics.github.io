# 9.5 Maturity self-assessment

Every chapter in Parts 1 through 8 ends with a five-level maturity model:
1 Initiate, 2 Develop, 3 Standardize, 4 Manage, 5 Orchestrate. This
appendix consolidates them into one matrix for organizational
self-assessment. Score each chapter honestly, using concrete evidence, not
aspiration. See chapter 8.4 for the cross-cutting, five-dimension programme
model this chapter-by-chapter matrix complements, and remember that
programme maturity is the minimum across dimensions, not the average.

## How to use this matrix

1. For each chapter, read its own maturity model (the chapter is the
   authoritative source; this table is a summary index).
2. Score your organization 1 through 5 against concrete evidence, not
   intention.
3. Do not average across chapters within a part; each chapter measures a
   distinct capability.
4. Feed low scores into chapter 8.5's adoption roadmap as investment
   priorities, not as a verdict to feel bad about (chapter 1.1).

# 9.0 Appendices

Part 9 collects the book's reference apparatus and practical toolkit: the
material you reach for while *doing* the work rather than reading straight
through. Where Parts 1 through 8 explain principles and recommendations,
these appendices give you the definitions, ready-to-use artefacts,
self-assessment, sources, and navigation that support them. Each is
written to stand alone, so you can link a team directly to a single
appendix without requiring them to have read the rest of the book first.

## Chapters in this part

- **9.1 Glossary**: Definitions of the terms and acronyms used across the
  book, from DORA and SPACE to SLO, MTTR, and Goodhart's law.
- **9.2 Metric definitions and formulas reference**: Every formula in the
  book, gathered in one place, with its precise definition and unit.
- **9.3 Checklists**: Ready-to-use quick-reference checklists for metric
  review, dashboard launch, and metrics programme audit.
- **9.4 Templates**: Copy-paste templates for a metrics charter, a
  dashboard specification, and a metric review agenda.
- **9.5 Maturity self-assessment**: The five-level maturity model from
  every chapter, consolidated into one matrix for organizational
  self-assessment, alongside chapter 8.4's cross-cutting programme model.
- **9.6 References and further reading**: A consolidated bibliography of
  the works cited throughout the book.
- **9.7 Index**: A subject index mapping key concepts to the chapters
  where they appear.

## How to use these appendices

Treat them as living companions to the chapters. When a chapter recommends
a practice, the corresponding appendix helps you act on it: chapter 1.4
(metrics governance) pairs with the metrics charter template in chapter
9.4; the security chapter (6.4) pairs with the vulnerability-severity
definitions in chapter 9.2; and the maturity section of every chapter feeds
the consolidated self-assessment in chapter 9.5. Start from a chapter's
recommendations, then use these appendices to turn them into artefacts,
assessments, and a rollout plan (chapter 8.5). Terms in any chapter are
defined in the glossary (chapter 9.1), and the index (chapter 9.7) helps
you find where a concept is treated in depth.
